About Our Institution

A Tradition of Academic Distinction & Moral Integrity

New Gulf Indian School (NGIS) stands as a beacon of educational excellence in Kuwait, dedicated to empowering young minds with knowledge, character, and global vision.

Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi, NGIS offers a comprehensive curriculum designed to spark intellectual curiosity, foster critical thinking, and build strong ethical values. Our campus provides state-of-the-art facilities, experienced educators, and a vibrant community environment.
